# Listening Notes

## V1 baseline
The listener reported that V1 (192 kHz-derived profile) offered:
- Slightly fuller bass than the earlier 48 kHz profile.
- Clear mid/high frequencies with controlled harshness.
- A perceived increase in airiness.
- Good balance between vocals and instruments.
- No obvious overall loudness reduction compared with the earlier version.

## V2-A initial cannon-effect test
After the Filters 1–7 gain changes, the listener reported:
- The cannon sound had a more defined, compact explosive core.
- The sound seemed to expand outward into a larger, less dense sphere.
- The perceived result felt more consistent with the auditory impression of artillery fire.

## Pending evaluation
The listener plans to revisit vocals and instruments to check for:
- Excess warmth or muddiness in male/female vocals.
- Masking of midrange detail.
- Excessive decay or looseness in bass instruments.
- Changes to piano low-register weight and percussion impact.

V2-A should remain experimental until this broader listening check is completed.
